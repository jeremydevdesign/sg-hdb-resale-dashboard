Contact

We read every message.

SG HDB Resale is run by one person as an independent project, and feedback from people using it is genuinely the best way it improves. Whether you have spotted a number that looks wrong, have an idea for something the site should show, or just have a question about how a figure is worked out, I'd like to hear from you.

Email

The best way to reach me is by email:

firenowsg@gmail.com

I usually reply within a few days. There is no contact form and no sign-up - a plain email is all it takes.

What's helpful to include

To help me look into things quickly, it's useful if you can mention:

  • Reporting a figure that looks off? Tell me which page you were on, and the town, flat type, and year range you had selected, so I can reproduce exactly what you saw.
  • Suggesting an improvement? A sentence on what you were trying to find out, and what would have helped, goes a long way.
  • Asking about the data? Let me know which number you're looking at - most questions are about how medians, MRT distance, or the year-to-date figures are calculated, which are explained on the About page.

A note on what I can help with

This is a data project, not a property agency. I can't value a specific flat, give buying, selling, or financial advice, or act on your behalf in any transaction - please see the Terms of Use for more on that. For questions about your own flat or an official transaction, HDB remains the authoritative source.

More about the site

If you're new here, the About page explains where the numbers come from and how to read them, and the Privacy Policy covers how your visit is handled.
