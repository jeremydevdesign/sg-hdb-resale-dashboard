About SG HDB Resale

SG HDB Resale is a free, independent project that turns a decade of Singapore's public housing resale data into something you can actually explore — whether you're buying your first flat, deciding when to sell, or just curious about where the market is going.

It started from a simple frustration: the official data is all there, but it's locked away in spreadsheets that are hard to make sense of. This site does the work of cleaning, joining, and summarising it, so you can ask real questions — what can I afford in this town? how much does living near an MRT cost? which districts are growing fastest? — and get an honest answer straight from the numbers.

Where the numbers come from

Every figure on this site comes from official, public sources:

  • Resale transactions — the full record of HDB resale flat sales published on data.gov.sg, covering every registered sale from 2012 onward.
  • Locations and MRT distances — flat blocks are geocoded and matched to the nearest MRT station using OneMap, Singapore's official map service.

The data is refreshed every month, shortly after data.gov.sg publishes the latest sales.

How to read the numbers

A few things worth knowing, so the figures mean what you think they mean:

  • Medians, not averages. Every price shown is a median — the middle of the range — which is far less skewed by a handful of unusually high or low sales than an average would be.
  • Distance to the MRT is straight-line. When you see "distance to MRT", it's the direct, as-the-crow-flies distance from the block to the nearest station — not walking distance, which is usually a little longer.
  • This year is year-to-date. The current year only includes sales registered so far, so it's a partial picture that fills in as the year goes on.
  • Small samples are shown, not hidden. Where only a few flats sold, the sale count is shown next to the median so you can judge how much weight to give it.

What this site is not

This is an independent, non-commercial project. It is not affiliated with the Housing & Development Board (HDB) or any Singapore government agency. The figures are for general information and interest only — they are not financial, legal, or property advice, and you shouldn't rely on them alone for a buying or selling decision.
