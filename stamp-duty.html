How this is calculated. Rates are the published IRAS figures as of 2026 - verify current rates at iras.gov.sg, as they change. Buyer's Stamp Duty (residential, from 15 Feb 2023) is tiered on the higher of price or market value: 1% on the first $180,000, 2% on the next $180,000, 3% on the next $640,000, 4% on the next $500,000, 5% on the next $1,500,000, and 6% above $3,000,000. Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (on or after 27 Apr 2023) is a flat percentage of the price by profile: Citizens 0% / 20% / 30% for the 1st / 2nd / 3rd+ property; PRs 5% / 30% / 35%; foreigners 60% on any. For a joint purchase, the highest applicable ABSD rate applies to the whole price. Figures are estimates, not tax advice or an assessment by IRAS. Rules last reviewed 2026-08-14.
